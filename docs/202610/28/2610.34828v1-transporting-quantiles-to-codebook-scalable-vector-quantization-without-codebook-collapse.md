## 摘要
基于VQ-VAE的有效离散表示学习是现代自回归生成模型的基础组成部分。然而，众所周知，它存在码本坍缩和码利用效率低的问题。为解决该问题，我们提出FlowVQ，一种简单而有效的标准向量量化替代方案，将神经量化表述为联合建模与聚类过程。具体而言，我们在训练过程中使用轻量级流匹配模型学习潜空间，这对应于一个概率流ODE，在保持概率质量的同时输运通常固定且性质良好的源分布。基于此，我们通过所学流将等分位数划分的先验码C_0从源空间输运，并在诱导分布下获得Voronoi质心，从而定义码本C_1，形成结构化的离散化。我们在图像分词与生成以及两个需要高精度分词的任务上进行了评估，展示了其可扩展性以及码本在充分利用时表达能力的提升。

## Abstract
Effective discrete representation learning with VQ-VAE serves as a fundamental component of modern autoregressive generative models. However, it is widely known to suffer from codebook collapse and inefficient code utilization. To address this issue, we propose FlowVQ, a simple yet effective alternative to standard vector quantization by formulating neural quantization as a joint modeling and clustering process. Specifically, we learn the latent space using a lightweight flow matching model alongside training, corresponding to a probability flow ODE that transports an often fixed well-behaved source distribution while preserving probability mass. Based on this, we define the codebook C_1 by transporting even-quantile partitioned prior codes C_0 from the source space through the learned flow, and obtaining the Voronoi centroids under the induced distribution, leading to a structured discretization. We evaluated on image tokenization and generation, as well as on two tasks requiring high-precision tokenization, demonstrating scalability and improved expressivity of the codebook with full utilization.
