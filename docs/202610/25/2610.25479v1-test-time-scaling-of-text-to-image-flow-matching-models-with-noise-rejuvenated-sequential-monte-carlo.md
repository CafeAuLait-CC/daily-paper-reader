## 摘要
利用序列蒙特卡洛（SMC）对扩散模型进行测试时扩展，已成为在用户指定奖励（如人类偏好分数）下采样的一种有前景的策略。然而，标准SMC框架通常依赖随机提议，因此不能直接迁移到流匹配模型，因为其推理由确定性ODE支配。在这项工作中，我们引入噪声重振SMC，这是一种专为文本到图像流匹配模型设计的推理时扩展方法。我们的关键思想是通过改变每个重采样粒子的源噪声，并利用其当前端点估计构造新的中间状态，从而对粒子进行扰动。这将粒子移动到另一个仍可在流匹配插值形式下表示的状态，使得无需将采样器转换为SDE即可实现粒子探索。我们进一步引入前瞻机制，在加权之前对重采样粒子进行细化。在Stable Diffusion 3、FLUX.1-dev和Z-Image上的实验表明，在奖励引导生成中取得了一致改进，同时消融研究验证了噪声重振和前瞻细化的贡献。代码见 https://github.com/shermanlian/NR-SMC。

## Abstract
Test-time scaling of diffusion models with sequential Monte Carlo (SMC) has become a promising strategy for sampling under user-specified rewards, such as human preference scores. However, the standard SMC framework typically relies on stochastic proposals and therefore does not directly transfer to flow matching models, in which inference is governed by deterministic ODEs. In this work, we introduce noise-rejuvenated SMC, an inference-time scaling method tailored for text-to-image flow matching models. Our key idea is to perturb each resampled particle by changing its source noise and constructing a new intermediate state using its current endpoint estimate. This moves the particle to another state that remains representable under the flow-matching interpolation form, enabling particle exploration without converting the sampler to an SDE. We further introduce a look-ahead mechanism to refine resampled particles before weighting. Experiments on Stable Diffusion 3, FLUX.1-dev, and Z-Image demonstrate consistent improvements in reward-guided generation, while ablation studies validate the contributions of noise rejuvenation and look-ahead refinement. Code is available at https://github.com/shermanlian/NR-SMC.
