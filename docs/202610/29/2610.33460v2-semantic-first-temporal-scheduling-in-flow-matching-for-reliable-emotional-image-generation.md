## 摘要
情感可控的图像生成应在改变场景感受的同时，保留提示词所指定的内容。我们研究流匹配模型中随机探索与策略优化的时间分配，以此作为控制这种平衡的手段。在强化学习训练过程中，我们的语义优先调度在高噪声阶段使用确定性常微分方程（ODE）采样，并将随机微分方程（SDE）探索与组相对策略优化（GRPO）限制在后期时间窗口内。受控实验将探索位置、优化位置和终端步采样分离，显示出后期探索和后期更新具有明显的语义收益。在 FLUX.1-dev 上，语义优先提升了提示词忠实度、独立评估的情感准确率，以及人工评定的语义保持度和整体偏好。扩展的语义审计记录到 2.7% 的主体漂移，同时相对于原始全路径 Flow-GRPO，GRPO 训练时间减少了 41.1%。窗口宽度实验刻画了情感-语义与计算之间的权衡，并在 SD3.5-Medium 上的复现实验重现了该调度优势。这些结果确立了去噪时间作为可靠情感图像生成的一个实用控制维度。

## Abstract
Affect-controlled image generation should change how a scene feels while preserving what the prompt specifies. We study the temporal allocation of stochastic exploration and policy optimization in flow-matching models as a means of controlling this balance. During RL training, our semantic-first schedule uses deterministic ordinary differential equation (ODE) sampling during the high-noise phase and confines stochastic differential equation (SDE) exploration and group relative policy optimization (GRPO) to a late window. Controlled experiments separate exploration location, optimization location, and terminal-step sampling, showing distinct semantic benefits from late exploration and late updates. On FLUX.1-dev, semantic-first improves prompt faithfulness, independently evaluated affect accuracy, and human-rated semantic preservation and overall preference. The expanded semantic audit records 2.7% subject drift, while GRPO training time decreases by 41.1% relative to original full-path Flow-GRPO. Window-width experiments characterize the affect-semantic and computational trade-offs, and replication on SD3.5-Medium reproduces the scheduling advantage. These results establish denoising time as a practical control dimension for reliable emotional image generation.
