## 摘要
流匹配通过回归学习一个生成速度场，但在分布漂移下，场必须在当前数据到达之前部署。我们研究固定（可能非凸）神经网络类下的这一预测问题，并以相对于每轮最佳可表示场的动态遗憾为指标。我们提出 DyProd-FM，它在二进历史前缀上重新拟合流匹配，并使用 sleeping Prod master 在多个拟合之间进行选择。在不知道任何漂移预算的情况下，它在相对速度风险变化 $Ω$ 上达到遗憾 $\widetilde{O}(\min\{N_ω+1,1+\sqrt{TΩ}\})$，在目标律变化 $V_μ=\sum_t W_2(μ_{t+1},μ_t)$ 上达到 $\widetilde{O}(\min\{N_μ+1,1+T^{1/3}V_μ^{2/3}\})$，直到近似与拟合代价。针对固定类的匹配下界表明，这两个速率在 $T$ 和预算上均最优（至多相差对数因子）。此前的动态遗憾最优保证依赖于具有强凸或 exp-concave 损失的凸决策集，而我们的类可能是非凸的，且不假设其参数具有曲率。目标律速率依赖于两种变化之间的二阶联系，这种联系由流匹配的基础噪声提供。

## Abstract
Flow matching learns a generative velocity field by regression, but under distribution drift a field must be deployed before the current data arrive. We study this predictive problem for a fixed, possibly nonconvex, neural class, with dynamic regret against the best represented field of each round. We propose DyProd-FM, which refits flow matching on dyadic historical prefixes and selects among the fits with a sleeping Prod master. Without knowing any drift budget, it attains regret $\widetilde{O}(\min\{N_ω+1,1+\sqrt{TΩ}\})$ in the relative velocity risk variation $Ω$ and $\widetilde{O}(\min\{N_μ+1,1+T^{1/3}V_μ^{2/3}\})$ in the target-law variation $V_μ=\sum_t W_2(μ_{t+1},μ_t)$, up to approximation and fitting costs. Matching lower bounds for fixed classes show that both rates are optimal in $T$ and in the budgets up to logarithmic factors. Prior optimal dynamic-regret guarantees rely on a convex decision set with strongly convex or exp-concave losses, whereas our class may be nonconvex and no curvature in its parameters is assumed. The target-law rate rests on a second-order link between the two variations, which the base noise of flow matching provides.
