## 摘要
运动序列中的稀有三维人体姿态可能反映真实运动，也可能反映估计误差；当参考数据库中可比较样本很少时，校正会变得困难。我们研究局部几何结构是否能够支持稀有构型的恢复与生成，使用 AMASS 中的动作捕捉序列（其通过统一的参数化人体模型整合多个数据集）以及基于视频的姿态估计。我们开发了圆柱流形注意力 Transformer（CMAT），通过依赖几何与任务的邻域权重扩展局部流形拟合。对于运动校正，Self-Action CMAT 利用序列内可比较的窗口，针对邻域不平衡和时间依赖性调整其贡献，并将局部几何与稳健的时间正则化相结合。在受扰动和估计的运动上的实验表明位置和加速度误差降低，并通过组件分析考察加权和时间处理如何促进恢复。受控校正比较是探索性的，并以所选运动幅度界限为条件。对于生成，保稀有性 CMAT 探索局部支持的构型，并受独立校准的稀有性以及与存储观测的分离度约束。在常见筛选标准下，其合格稀有姿态的产出率高于所评估的生成基线。

## Abstract
A rare 3D human pose in a motion sequence may reflect either a genuine movement or an estimation error, making correction difficult when reference databases contain few comparable examples. We investigate whether local geometric structure can support the recovery and generation of rare configura- tions, using motion-capture sequences from AMASS, which unifies datasets through a common parametric human-body model, together with video-based pose estimates. We develop the cylindrical manifold attention transformer (CMAT), which extends local manifold fitting through geometry- and task- dependent neighborhood weights. For motion correction, Self-Action CMAT draws on comparable windows within a sequence, adjusts their contributions for neighborhood imbalance and temporal dependence, and integrates local geometry with robust temporal regularization. Experiments on perturbed and estimated motions show reductions in position and acceleration errors, with component analyses examining how weighting and temporal processing con- tribute to recovery. The controlled correction comparisons are exploratory and conditional on the selected motion-amplitude bound. For generation, Rarity-preserving CMAT explores locally supported configurations subject to independently calibrated rarity and separation from stored observations. Under common screening criteria, it achieves a higher yield of eligible rare poses than the evaluated generation baselines.
