## 摘要
我们考虑在满足预设的逐样本约束、且约束满足概率由用户指定的情况下，学习生成数据的问题。我们从总体层面的约束负对数似然最小化出发，并采用流匹配（FM）参数化，在正则性假设以及速度场族具有足够表达能力的前提下，推导了相应原对偶优化算法在约束满足概率上的保证。我们给出了该方法的实际实现，称为原对偶流匹配（PDFM），它可直接控制约束满足率，并且仅需用于约束满足的二元成员关系预言机反馈，从而避免了对可微距离、投影或约束集特殊结构（如凸性）的需求。我们表明，PDFM 相较于普通 FM 提升了约束满足程度，并在保持分布保真度的同时，相对于约束感知基线取得了有竞争力的性能。我们的代码可在 https://github.com/ZhengyanHuan/PDFM 获取。

## Abstract
We consider the problem of learning to generate data while satisfying a prescribed \emph{sample-wise} constraint with a user-specified probability of constraint satisfaction. Starting with a population-level constrained negative log-likelihood minimization and using a flow-matching (FM) parameterization, under regularity assumptions and sufficient expressivity of the velocity field family, we derive guarantees on the probability of constraint satisfaction for the corresponding primal-dual optimization algorithm. We provide a practical implementation of this approach, called Primal-Dual Flow Matching (PDFM), which has direct control over constraint satisfaction rate and requires only binary membership oracle feedback for constraint satisfaction, avoiding the need for differentiable distances, projections, or special structure of the constraint set, such as convexity. We show that PDFM improves constraint satisfaction over vanilla FM and achieves competitive performance against constraint-aware baselines while maintaining distributional fidelity. Our code is available at https://github.com/ZhengyanHuan/PDFM.
