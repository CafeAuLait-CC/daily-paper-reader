## 摘要
生成模型为科学模拟与发现提供了强大途径，使得能够研究复杂群体和稀有现象。流匹配可以生成逼真的样本，但发生频率不准确会扭曲群体统计量并损害对稀有事件的推断。在此，我们开发了广义质量保持耦合流匹配（G-MPCF），它将高斯源区域的概率质量与加权数据区域的概率质量相匹配。该构造在噪声与观测之间的对应关系中编码了所需的区域频率，从而在保持规定目标分布的同时塑造学习到的动力学。在稀有模式基准上，与独立流匹配相比，G-MPCF 将相对分数误差降低 95.0%，将数值积分误差降低 89.9%。在类别概率比为 100:1 的 CIFAR-10 上，与小批量最优传输相比，它将校正后的类别质量误差降低 44.6%，同时在计算预算匹配的情况下提高平均图像保真度。我们设想，将区域概率校准纳入生成建模将支持更可靠的科学模拟与推断，使得能够研究那些稀有事件及其发生频率对理解底层机制至关重要的系统。

## Abstract
Generative models offer a powerful route to scientific simulation and discovery by enabling the study of complex populations and rare phenomena. Flow matching can generate realistic samples, yet inaccurate occurrence frequencies can distort population statistics and compromise inferences about rare events. Here we developed generalized mass-preserving coupled flow matching (G-MPCF), which matches the probability masses of Gaussian source regions to those of weighted data regions. This construction encodes the desired regional frequencies in the correspondence between noise and observations, shaping the learned dynamics while preserving the prescribed target distribution. On a rare-mode benchmark, G-MPCF reduces relative score error by 95.0% and numerical integration error by 89.9% compared with independent flow matching. On CIFAR-10 with a 100:1 class-probability ratio, it reduces corrected class-mass error by 44.6% compared with minibatch optimal transport, while improving mean image fidelity with matched computational budgets. We envision that incorporating regional probability calibration into generative modelling will support more reliable scientific simulation and inference, enabling the study of systems in which rare events and their occurrence frequencies are central to understanding the underlying mechanisms.
