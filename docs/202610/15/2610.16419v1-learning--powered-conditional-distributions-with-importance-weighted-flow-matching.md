## 摘要
具有引导尺度 $γ$ 的无分类器引导（CFG）通常以令 $ω=γ$ 所得到的 $ω$ 次幂条件分布为动机，但 CFG 通常并不能精确地从该分布中采样。我们研究能否转而显式地学习该幂目标分布。我们提出重要性加权条件流匹配（IW-CFM），它利用从冻结的条件流模型和无条件流模型中获得的密度比对条件流匹配样本进行重加权。在精确归一化权重下，所得目标函数与在幂目标分布上进行流匹配具有相同梯度；在实践中，我们使用自归一化估计以及针对高维数据的正则化经验近似。在合成分布上，IW-CFM 能够恢复解析的幂目标分布，而数值匹配的 CFG（$γ=ω$）在这些目标上表现出明显的不匹配。在 ImageNet 上，摊销的轻量级校正器与相应的加权和滤波参考分布之间的对齐优于固定引导和匹配引导的 CFG。我们额外展示了对真实 ImageNet 数据上幂密度排序的探索性 CLIP 分析。在选定的提示特定对比中，总体偏移与异质的逐类响应并存，包括非单调轨迹。这些结果为研究和近似模型定义的幂条件分布提供了一条实用途径。

## Abstract
Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) with guidance scale $γ$ is commonly motivated by the $ω$-powered conditional distribution obtained by setting $ω=γ$, but CFG does not, in general, sample from this distribution exactly. We study whether the powered target can instead be learned explicitly. We introduce Importance-Weighted Conditional Flow Matching (IW-CFM), which reweights conditional flow-matching examples using density ratios obtained from frozen conditional and unconditional flow models. With exact normalized weights, the resulting objective has the same gradient as flow matching on the powered target; in practice, we use self-normalized estimation and a regularized empirical approximation for high-dimensional data. On synthetic distributions, IW-CFM recovers analytical powered targets for which numerically matched CFG, $γ=ω$, exhibits a visible mismatch. On ImageNet, an amortized lightweight corrector achieves better alignment than fixed- and matched-guidance CFG with the corresponding weighted and filtered reference distributions. We additionally present an exploratory CLIP analysis of powered-density rankings on real ImageNet data. Aggregate shifts across selected prompt-specific contrasts coexist with heterogeneous classwise responses, including nonmonotonic trajectories. These results provide a practical route for studying and approximating model-defined powered conditional distributions.
