## 摘要
具身说话人的表现力不仅取决于说了什么，还取决于声音听起来如何以及身体如何运动。然而，现有的语音-动作联合生成模型在很大程度上由转录文本语义或中间语音表征隐式驱动，对动作层级、身体部位或声音风格提供的显式控制很少。为弥合这一差距，我们构建了 MotionSpeech，一个用于可控语音-动作联合生成的结构化基准，包含转录文本、声音风格描述以及两个层级上的动作条件。基于该基准，我们提出 DuoFlow，一种双流潜在流 Transformer，在共享噪声水平和统一的基于秒的时间坐标下联合去噪语音与动作。模态特定的残差连接器将结构化条件注入对应流中，逐层双向跨模态注意力协调二者的生成，单调音素路径将归一化进度锚点与 CTC 监督相结合，以增强语音时间建模。DuoFlow 为细粒度动作和声音风格控制提供了统一接口，并展示了合成高质量、同步且与指令一致的语音-动作序列的能力。项目页面：https://jundong-dev.github.io/DuoFlow-project-page。

## Abstract
An embodied speaker's expressiveness depends not only on what is said, but also on how the voice sounds and how the body moves. However, existing joint speech-motion generation models are largely driven implicitly by transcript semantics or intermediate speech representations, offering little explicit control over motion hierarchy, body parts, or vocal style. To bridge this gap, we construct MotionSpeech, a structured benchmark for controllable joint speech-motion generation that includes transcripts, voice-style descriptions, and motion conditions at two hierarchical levels. Building on this benchmark, we propose DuoFlow, a dual-stream latent flow transformer that jointly denoises speech and motion under a shared noise level and a unified second-based temporal coordinate. Modality-specific residual connectors inject structured conditions into the corresponding streams, layer-wise bidirectional cross-modal attention coordinates their generation, and a monotonic phoneme pathway combines normalized progress anchors with CTC supervision to strengthen speech temporal modeling. DuoFlow provides a unified interface for fine-grained motion and vocal-style control and demonstrates the ability to synthesize high-quality, synchronized, and instruction-consistent speech-motion sequences. Project page, https://jundong-dev.github.io/DuoFlow-project-page.
